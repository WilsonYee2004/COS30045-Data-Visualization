TV Brand Market Distribution

Understanding the landscape of appliance manufacturers is essential for analyzing market trends. This visualization explores the distribution of television brands within our dataset, rendered dynamically using D3.js.

Market Presence by Manufacturer

The horizontal bar chart below illustrates the total count of television models available for each brand. Hover over the bars to interact with the data.

As the data clearly shows, Samsung, Kogan, and LG dominate the television market in this dataset. This heavy concentration among a few key manufacturers suggests a highly consolidated market where consumer choices and energy consumption baselines are primarily driven by these leading brands.
