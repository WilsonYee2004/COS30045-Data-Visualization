TV Energy Consumption Dashboard

Welcome to our interactive energy analysis tool. As television screens grow larger and panel technologies evolve, understanding their power consumption is crucial for both your electricity bill and the environment. Use the tools below to explore the data.

1. Frequency Distribution by Technology

Click the buttons below to filter the histogram. Notice how LED TVs dominate the market share in this dataset, while OLED TVs show a tighter, more specific energy footprint.

The histogram reveals that the vast majority of televisions consume between 200 and 400 kWh annually. However, extreme outliers exist within the dataset, with some models consuming upwards of 1,000 kWh per year, heavily impacting household energy efficiency.

2. Efficiency vs. Star Rating

Hover over the data points to reveal specific screen sizes. This scatterplot reveals the correlation between a TV's official energy star rating and its actual energy consumption.

As observed in the scatterplot, higher star ratings generally correlate with lower energy consumption, but screen size plays a massive role. A highly-rated 85-inch TV might still use more power than a lower-rated 32-inch model, proving that physical screen size is the ultimate determining factor in overall power draw.
